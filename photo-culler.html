Photo Culler · Demo

Cull a shoot in one pass

Point it at a folder of frames. It finds the bursts, scores every frame for sharpness and exposure, and keeps the best one from each. What took an hour of squinting takes about four seconds.

About this demo. The shipping tool is Python and runs CLIP embeddings over libraries of 10,000+ photos. This page is the same pipeline with a smaller brain: perceptual hashing for near-duplicate clustering and Laplacian variance for sharpness, all in your browser. The frames loaded below are real — a sideline sequence from a football shoot, September 2024. Drop your own in and it will cull those instead; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What it actually does

1 · Fingerprint

Each frame is reduced to 9×8 greyscale and turned into a 64-bit difference hash. Two frames of the same moment land within a few bits of each other.

2 · Cluster

Union-find over every pair whose Hamming distance falls under the threshold. Bursts collapse into one group; singles stay single.

3 · Score

Laplacian variance for sharpness, plus a penalty for clipped highlights and crushed shadows. Motion blur and missed focus sink to the bottom.

4 · Pick

Highest score in each cluster is the keeper. Everything else is cut, and you can override any call with a click.

The keep list

What you would feed to Lightroom. Updates as you override picks.
