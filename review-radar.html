Review Radar · Demo

Two messy APIs, one weekly digest

Reviews arrive from different platforms in different shapes, on different rating scales, with different date formats and half the fields missing. Review Radar pulls them, makes them agree, finds what people are complaining about, and tells you when a complaint is growing.

About this demo. The shipping service is Python and FastAPI, pulling two live review APIs on a schedule and persisting to PostgreSQL. This page runs the same normalization, tagging and alerting logic in your browser against synthetic sample reviews written for this demo — no real guest data, and the two endpoints are simulated so you can watch the retry layer work. Everything downstream of the fetch is the real logic.

4 · What's moving
